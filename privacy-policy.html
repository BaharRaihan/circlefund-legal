CircleFund Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 October 2026. Last updated: 22 October 2026.

CircleFund ("the app", "we", "us") is a record-keeping app for savings groups (called samiti, chama, stokvel, susu, tontine and similar names in different places). It is published by Bahar Uddin Raihan. You can reach us at support.circle.fund@gmail.com.

CircleFund only keeps records. It does not hold, send or lend money, and it never asks for your bank card, card number or bank password.

1. Who is responsible for which data

Your savings group (its president, general secretary and other officers) decides what is recorded about the group: members, payments, loans, investments, expenses, notices and meetings. We provide the app and the servers that store this information on the group's behalf. For your own account details (name, email, sign-in), we are the controller.

3. Who can see your information

  • Inside your group: by default every active member can see the group's records, including other members' payments and loans (this is the transparency rule of most savings groups). The president or general secretary can switch this off, and then ordinary members see only their own entries and the group's totals. Officers, committee members and the auditor see everything.
  • Other groups and the public: cannot see your group's records.
  • Us: the developer account can reach the stored data for support, security and to run the service, and uses this only for those purposes.

5. How long we keep data

Group records are kept for as long as the group uses the app, because savings groups need their history. If you delete your account (see section 7), your personal details are removed or replaced by "Former member" while the group's money records stay, so the group's books remain complete. If the only member of a group deletes the account and chooses to delete the whole group, all of that group's records are deleted. Backups held by Google may take some time to expire.

6. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Access is controlled by security rules on the server so that only people the group allows can read or change a record, and important entries cannot be edited or deleted, only reversed with a visible entry. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and keep your phone locked.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: in the app, open More, scroll to "Danger zone" and choose "Delete my account". You can also follow the steps on our account deletion page or email us from your registered address.
  • Correct your name: More, "My profile".
  • See or get a copy of your data, correct it, object or complain: email us. Group records belong to the group, so we may ask the group's officers to take part in your request. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.
  • Notifications: you can switch categories off in the app, or in your phone's settings.
  • Ads: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Free text written by officers (notes, meeting minutes, activity log sentences) may still contain a person's name after the account is deleted, because changing it would alter the group's record.

8. Children

CircleFund is for adults. It is not directed to children under 18 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has an account, email us and we will remove it.

9. International transfers

Google may process data in countries other than yours. Google applies its own safeguards for these transfers.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
