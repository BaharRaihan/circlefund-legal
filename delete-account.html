Delete your CircleFund account

You can delete your CircleFund account and the personal data attached to it at any time.

Option 1: in the app (fastest)

  1. Open CircleFund and sign in.
  2. Go to More and scroll to the bottom, to Danger zone.
  3. Tap Delete my account.
  4. Read what will be deleted, type your password again, and confirm.

If you are not in a savings group yet, the same button is on the "Let's get you started" screen.

Option 2: you cannot use the app

Email support.circle.fund@gmail.com from the email address of your account with the subject "Delete my CircleFund account". We will check that the request comes from you and delete the account, normally within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account (email and password).
  • Your user profile, your notification inbox and your push notification tokens.
  • Your name, phone and email on the group's records are replaced by "Former member" (or removed).

What is kept

  • The group's money records (payments, loans, expenses, month closings and the activity log) stay, so that the group's books remain complete and correct for the other members. They no longer show your name.
  • Free text written by officers (notes, meeting minutes) may still mention your name.
  • If you are the only member and choose "also delete the whole savings group", all of that group's records are deleted.

When the app asks you to do something first

  • You are the president and other members are active: hand over the presidency first (Members, choose a member, "Make president").
  • You owe an active loan: the committee must settle or close the loan first.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it in Google Play (Play Store, profile, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions) so you are not charged again.
